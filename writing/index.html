A quarter of what Armenia sends Russia is now electronics

UN Comtrade August 2026

Armenian electronics exports to Russia rose from nine million dollars to eight hundred million. The dual-use share rose in all eight country and chapter pairs tested. Direct German supply cannot account for the volume, which turns the question from whether this is transshipment into where the goods are actually coming from.
