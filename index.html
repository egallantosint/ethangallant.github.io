MedGraph

A dependency cascade model of the Eastern Mediterranean, Red Sea and South Caucasus, in which every edge is labelled as measured or analyst assigned, every judgement carries a recorded falsifier, and sensitivity analysis identifies which assumption a conclusion actually rests on.

Measuring three edges against IMF PortWatch and UN Comtrade did not confirm the earlier finding. It promoted the weakest assumption in the register to load-bearing. Silence the judgements altogether and a Suez disruption reaches one node, which the tool reports rather than hides.

Commercial Infrastructure, Strategic Dependence

Chinese state-linked ownership across eleven Mediterranean container terminals, assessed against NATO logistical exposure. Confidence graded separately for commercial influence, military utility and evidence of operational use, because the evidence behind each is qualitatively different.

When the Red Sea closed in 2024 and Piraeus throughput fell 7.8 per cent, the traffic reappeared at Valencia and Zeebrugge. It never left the COSCO network. Dependence sits at the level of the corporate perimeter, not the port.

Analysis · UN Comtrade

A quarter of what Armenia sends Russia is now electronics

Armenian electronics exports to Russia rose from nine million dollars to eight hundred million. The dual-use share rose in all eight country and chapter pairs tested. Direct German supply cannot account for the volume, which turns the question from whether this is transshipment into where the goods actually come from.
