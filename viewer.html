MEDGRAPH

Eastern Mediterranean, Red Sea and South Caucasus dependency cascade
This is not a forecast. It makes a set of stated assumptions computable so their consequences can be traced and tested. The output that matters is not a score, it is a ranking plus the assumption that would have to change to reorder it.

What this is

A directed graph of who depends on whom across the Eastern Mediterranean, Red Sea and South Caucasus. States, alliances, ports, gas fields and chokepoints, connected by dependencies rather than by geography.

Every connection carries a weight and a provenance. Some are measured from trade and shipping data with a cited source. The rest are analyst judgements, because alliance strength and political friction cannot be measured, and each is declared with a falsifier attached rather than buried inside a number.

What it does

Choose somewhere a disruption starts. The model pushes it outward along the dependency edges, weakening at each step, and reports who is reached, in what order, and by how much.

Then it does the part that matters. It takes each stated assumption, pushes it to its plausible bounds in both directions and re-runs. An assumption that changes nothing can be held loosely. One that reorders the table is where the analysis actually rests, and the tool tells you which is which.

How to use it

  1. Pick a shock origin on the left.
  2. Read the cascade under the map. Order is how many steps away a node sits.
  3. Click any node, on the map or in the table, for every route the shock took to reach it and the arithmetic behind its number.
  4. Drag an assumption on the right and watch the ranking reorder. Arrows show how far each node moved.
  5. Toggle MEASURED EVIDENCE ONLY to silence every judgement and see what the evidence alone carries.
